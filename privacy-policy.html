CryptoLearn Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide and improve our cryptocurrency education service:

  • Email Address: For account creation and communication
  • Name: For personalization of your learning experience
  • Learning Progress: To track your course completion and achievements
  • Device Information: To optimize app performance and compatibility
  • Usage Analytics: To understand how you use our app and improve features
  • Technical Data: Error logs and crash reports to fix bugs

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Account Management: To create and maintain your user account
  • Personalization: To customize your learning path and recommendations
  • Progress Tracking: To show your course completion and achievements
  • Analytics: To improve app functionality and user experience
  • Support: To respond to your questions and provide assistance
  • Security: To protect your account and prevent unauthorized access

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes, except as described in this policy:

  • Service Providers: We may share anonymized data with trusted service providers for app functionality
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: Information may be transferred if we are acquired or merge with another company
  • Analytics Services: We use Google Analytics with anonymized data

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access Controls: Only authorized personnel can access your data
  • Regular Updates: We maintain and update security measures
  • Secure Storage: Your data is stored on secure servers

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You can access and update your personal information
  • Correction: You can correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability: You can request a copy of your data
  • Opt-out: You can opt out of certain data collection
  • Complaint: You can file complaints with data protection authorities

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such information, we will delete it immediately.

7. International Users

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We take appropriate safeguards to ensure your data remains protected in accordance with applicable laws, including:

  • GDPR: For users in the European Union
  • CCPA: For users in California
  • CalOPPA: For users in California

8. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Email: Sending notifications to your registered email
  • In-App Notice: Displaying prominent notices in the app
  • Website Posting: Updating the policy on our website

CryptoLearn Privacy Policy
========================

Effective Date: January 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect
- Email address (for account creation and communication)
- Name (for personalization)
- Learning progress and course completion data
- Device information for app optimization
- Usage analytics via Google Analytics
- Technical data for bug fixes

2. How We Use Your Information
- Account management and authentication
- Personalized learning experience
- Progress tracking and achievements
- App improvement through analytics
- Customer support and assistance
- Security and fraud prevention

3. Information Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes, except as required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Data Security
- Data encryption in transit and at rest
- Access controls and authentication
- Regular security updates
- Secure server storage

5. Your Rights
- Access and update your personal information
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request data deletion
- Opt out of data collection
- File complaints with authorities

6. Children's Privacy
Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. International Users
Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We take appropriate safeguards to ensure your data remains protected in accordance with applicable laws, including:
- GDPR (European Union)
- CCPA (California)
- CalOPPA (California)

8. Policy Updates
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:
- Email notifications to your registered email
- In-app notices
- Website updates

9. Contact Information
If you have questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us:
- Email: privacy@cryptolearn.me
- Website: https://cryptolearn.me
- Response Time: Within 30 days
